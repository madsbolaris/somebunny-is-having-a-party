# B4 Theme Analysis — Somebunny is Having a Party

> Every card in the Bracket 4 deck evaluated for theme fit with **"Somebunny is Having a Party"** — a Baylen, the Haymaker rabbit tribal token-combo deck.

### Scoring Guide
- **10**: Perfect — rabbit + party + core synergy
- **7–9**: Strong — clearly on-theme (rabbit or party flavor + good synergy)
- **4–6**: Moderate — functional connection to the party
- **1–3**: Weak — pure power card, minimal flavor connection

### Key Takeaways

- **10 of 90 unique cards are Rabbits** by creature type — the tribal core is tight and focused.
- **17 cards feature rabbit art** — almost all Bloomburrow (BLB) originals.
- **48 of 90 cards have a party connection** — over half the deck tells the "Somebunny is Having a Party" story through name, flavor, or function.
- **Highest theme density**: Creatures (avg 6.9) and Sorceries (avg 5.4) — the rabbits and their party activities carry the theme.
- **Lowest theme density**: Lands (avg 2.9) — necessary mana infrastructure, but The Shire and Cavern of Souls are standout thematic lands.
- **Pure power cards with minimal theme**: Sol Ring, Chrome Mox, Arcane Signet, Skullclamp, Sylvan Library, Survival of the Fittest, Enlightened Tutor — these are here because they win games, not because they're invited to the party.
