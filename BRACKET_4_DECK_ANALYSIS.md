## PHASE 1: Strategic Foundation Assessment

### 1.1 Archetype Identification

**Primary Strategy Type**: Tribal / Go-Wide Tokens — same identity as B3, now with a **2-card combo finish**.

**Primary Win Conditions**:
1. **Natural Order → Craterhoof Behemoth** turn 2–4 (sac a Hare Apparent for Craterhoof = game-winning overrun).
2. **Survival of the Fittest engine** — discard a spare Hare Apparent (you have 11 more!) to tutor any creature; pairs with Natural Order and finds Craterhoof, Endurance, Welcoming Vampire, ramp dorks.
3. **Go-wide combat overrun** with token doublers + anthems + Craterhoof / Finale of Devastation.
4. **Halo Fountain alt-win** (now reachable via Enlightened Tutor).

**Commander's Role**: **Important → Supportive**. Natural Order line wins without ever casting Baylen.

**Coherence**: Even tighter than current — every Game Changer doubles down on speed or tutor-redundancy.

---

### 1.2 Speed and Consistency

**Average Win Turn (goldfish)**: **Turn 4–6**, with T3 wins on nut hands (Mox Diamond + Sol Ring + Hare Apparent + Natural Order → Craterhoof).

**Game Stage Capabilities**:
- **T1**: Mox Diamond / Chrome Mox / Sol Ring / Birds of Paradise / Hare Apparent + Ancient Tomb opens explosive plays.
- **T2**: Bloom Tender or Three Visits → multiple permanents per turn; Survival of the Fittest can flip a Hare Apparent into a finisher already.
- **T3–4**: Natural Order → Craterhoof; or Tempt with Bunnies + a doubler for absurd token count; or Enlightened Tutor → Cathars' Crusade / Smothering Tithe.
- **T5+**: Generally already won.

**Consistency**:
- **Tutors: 9** (Chord of Calling, Eladamri's Call, Enlightened Tutor, Green Sun's Zenith, Natural Order, Finale of Devastation, **Survival of the Fittest**, Sylvan Library top-deck control). Survival is *repeatable*, which is the biggest jump.
- Hare Apparent ×12 + token doublers + anthems unchanged → identical massive redundancy.
- Mulligan quality: extremely forgiving — Mox Diamond + Chrome Mox enable more keeps than the B3 build.

---

### 1.3 Game Plan Documentation

**Ideal Sequence**:
- **T1**: Land + Mox Diamond + Sol Ring + Hare Apparent + Birds of Paradise.
- **T2**: Ancient Tomb + Bloom Tender / Hare Apparent #2; or cast Survival of the Fittest, immediately pitch a Hare Apparent for Craterhoof in hand.
- **T3**: **Natural Order → Craterhoof Behemoth** sacrificing a Hare Apparent → game-winning swing with Birds + tokens + Hare Apparent on board.
- **T4–5**: If not winning, Smothering Tithe + Cathars' Crusade + Tempt with Bunnies rebuild and finish.

**Critical Mass**: Just **one rabbit + 3 mana** for Natural Order line. Vastly lower than the B3 deck's "8 rabbits + anthem" requirement.

---

### Phase 1 Strategy Summary

The Bracket 4 build converts the rabbit-tribal go-wide plan into a **fringe-cEDH combo-tribal hybrid** that wins on **turns 4–6**, sometimes turn 3. **Natural Order → Craterhoof Behemoth** sacrificing any Hare Apparent is a 2-card "compact combo" that ends games on the spot, supported by **9 tutors** (including the repeatable Survival of the Fittest), **5+ sub-2-CMC fast mana** (Sol Ring, Mox Diamond, Chrome Mox, Ancient Tomb, Birds), and the unchanged 12-Hare-Apparent token engine. **Teferi's Protection** turns the win turn into a near-uninterruptible swing. Primary weakness is no stack interaction (mono-Naya), making it vulnerable to fast cEDH counter decks but excellent into Bracket 4 / Power 8–9 tables.

## Final Summary

**Key Strengths**:
1. **Survival + Natural Order + Craterhoof engine** with 9 tutors finding pieces.
2. **Premium 100% Tier 1–2 mana base**: Triple ABUR duals, in-color fetches, Ancient Tomb, Gaea's Cradle, Mox Diamond, Chrome Mox.
3. **Teferi's Protection + Aura Shards** — best-in-format white protection + permanent removal package.

**Key Weaknesses**:
1. **No stack interaction** (mono-Naya) — true cEDH ceiling capped here.
2. **Single primary combo line** — Natural Order → Craterhoof is the only sub-T5 win.
3. **Bracket 4's banned-list cap** — Mana Crypt and Jeweled Lotus are banned, so 0-CMC density is permanently limited to Mox Diamond + Chrome Mox.

## Additional On-Theme Swap Recommendations (B4-Legal, No Banned Cards)

These suggestions stay rabbit/token themed, are Bracket 4 legal, and avoid the banned list (Mana Crypt, Jeweled Lotus, Dockside Extortionist, Nadu, Sway of the Stars are all banned in B4).

### High-confidence (clear upgrade)

| Cut | Add | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Bloom Tender** | **Wood Elves** | Bloom Tender taps WG only in mono-Naya (Baylen has no red). Wood Elves is creature ramp that fetches Stomping Ground / Sacred Foundry untapped, AND is Natural Order / Survival / Chord food. Strict on-theme upgrade. *(NOT a Game Changer.)* |
| **Jacked Rabbit** | **Bennie Bracks, Zoologist** | Bennie IS a rabbit, draws on every 1/1 token entering — fuels the engine. Survival can tutor it. *(NOT a Game Changer.)* |

### Medium-confidence (consider for the meta)

| Cut | Add | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Endurance** | **Scavenging Ooze** | Endurance is excellent flash GY hate. Scav Ooze is a 1-CMC creature that grows over time, repeatable graveyard exile, Natural Order target, Survival target. Trade flash + reach for permanence + scaling. |
| **Carrot Cake** | **Heraldic Banner (rabbit)** | Carrot Cake is slow draw/food. Heraldic Banner is anthem + ramp for rabbits, repeatable. *(User has previously declined cutting Carrot Cake — leaving as a flag.)* |
| **Pollen-Shield Hare** | **Esper Sentinel** | (User vetoed Esper Sentinel as "creepy" — leaving as flag, but it's the strongest 1-CMC draw engine in white.) |

### Adding an 11th Game Changer (optional, still Bracket 4)

| Add | Replaces | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Gamble** *(Game Changer)* | Pollen-Shield Hare OR Jacked Rabbit | 1R sorcery that tutors any card — finds Craterhoof / Natural Order / Survival of the Fittest / Cathars' Crusade. Random discard rarely matters with 12 Hare Apparent in deck. Adding it puts you at 11 Game Changers, well within Bracket 4. |
| **Worldly Tutor** *(Game Changer)* | (re-add as a creature tutor) | Instant-speed creature top-of-deck tutor; finds Craterhoof at end of opponent's turn before your combo turn. Game Changer count → 11. |

### Recommended priority order

If you only do two more swaps:
1. **−Bloom Tender / +Wood Elves** (strict ramp upgrade, on-theme creature, Natural Order food)
2. **−Jacked Rabbit / +Bennie Bracks, Zoologist** (more on-theme creature, draws cards, Survival-tutorable)

*(Keep Shamanic Revelation — in this token-heavy deck it draws far more than Return of the Wildspeaker, which only counts highest-power creatures and would exclude all your 1/1 rabbit tokens.)*
