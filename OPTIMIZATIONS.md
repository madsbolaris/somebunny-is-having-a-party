# Bracket 4 Deck Optimizations

## Overview
This document proposes key improvements to the Bracket 4 "Somebunny is Having a Party" decklist, focusing on mana acceleration, redundancy reduction, and card advantage consistency while maintaining strong thematic elements.

## Proposed Changes

### 1. **Mana Acceleration — Add Mox Opal** (Net +0 slots)
**Remove:** Chrome Mox (theme: 1/10)  
**Add:** Mox Opal (theme: 1/10, but much stronger effect)

**Rationale:**
- Chrome Mox is the weakest mana producer in the deck (pure pitch, no recursion or utility)
- Mox Opal untaps and generates value in artifact-heavy builds
- With 10+ artifacts (Skullclamp, Sol Ring, Arcane Signet, Signet, and 7+ others), Mox Opal becomes a turn-1 play that enables explosive early sequences
- Both are low-theme, but Opal is far superior mechanically

---

### 2. **Add Green Sun's Zenith** (Net -1 cards)
**Remove:** Worldly Tutor (sorcery speed, single-use tutor)  
**Add:** Green Sun's Zenith (instant speed, repeatable from graveyard, ramp)

**Rationale:**
- Green Sun's Zenith tutors creatures *and* ramps (searching for a mana dork like Birds of Paradise)
- Instant speed means it can be cast in response to threats
- Returns to hand allowing repeated casting for consistency
- Worldly Tutor is good but strictly worse in this shell — it only puts the creature on top, doesn't guarantee it resolves
- Zenith is one of the most powerful tutors in green; higher impact

---

### 3. **Reduce Token Doubling Redundancy** (Net -1 cards)
**Remove:** Parallel Lives (theme: 5/10, least impactful of the three doublers)  
**Add:** Abundance (theme: 5/10, different effect — stops flooding)

**Rationale:**
- Three identical doubling effects (Anointed Procession, Parallel Lives, Doubling Season) is excessive redundancy
- Parallel Lives is the slowest to set up (5 mana, enchantment)
- Abundance solves a real problem in token-combo decks: mana flooding
  - Lets you choose to draw a card instead of a land
  - Turns extra land draws into gas mid-combo
  - Synergizes with Sylvan Library (already in B3, could add to B4)
- Keeps token doubling coverage while improving consistency

---

### 4. **Add Fauna Shaman — Repeatable Tutor** (Net -1 cards)
**Remove:** Enlightened Tutor (theme: 2/10, tutors only enchantments/artifacts)  
**Add:** Fauna Shaman (theme: 7/10, a rabbit, flicker-synergy)

**Rationale:**
- Fauna Shaman is a creature (triggers all your lord effects and token doublers)
- Repeatable tutor engine (discard a creature, search one)
- Works with Survival of the Fittest to chain tutors
- Flickers synergize with Ephemerate/Soulwind Djinn strategies
- Is literally a rabbit — better thematic fit than generic tutor
- Enlightened Tutor is good but narrow (only enchantments/artifacts); Fauna Shaman hits any creature

---

### 5. **Improve Combat Evasion — Add Ephemerate** (Net -1 cards)
**Remove:** Boros Charm (theme: 3/10, generic protection)  
**Add:** Ephemerate (theme: 5/10, flicker synergy, evasion)

**Rationale:**
- Ephemerate blinks one creature to dodge removal and triggers all enter-the-battlefield effects
- Works with Warren Warleader, Head of the Homestead, Harvestrite Host, Byrke, Crathoof bounce-back, etc.
- Instant speed pseudo-protection without the high mana requirement
- Can be foretold for 0 mana (suspend 1) for repeatable evasion each turn
- Boros Charm is good (triple-mode) but Teferi's Protection already handles board wipes
- Ephemerate fills a different niche: targeted creature protection with upside

---

### 6. **Add Skullclamp Synergy — Consider Toski** (Flexible)
**Option A — Minor:** Keep Skullclamp as-is  
**Option B — Strategic:** Add Toski, Bearer of Secrets (draw engine)

**Rationale (if added):**
- Toski is a 2/2 creature with evasion
- Every creature you control that deals damage draws a card
- With 10+ Hares, this is a draw engine
- Doesn't need Skullclamp to shine (unlike pure 1/1 synergies)
- Could replace Path to Exile or a land if going this direction
- Better than pure removal in a token deck

## Summary of Changes

| Slot | Out | In | Impact |
|------|-----|--|----|
| 1 | Chrome Mox | Mox Opal | +Acceleration consistency |
| 2 | Worldly Tutor | Green Sun's Zenith | +Instant speed, ramp, repeatable |
| 3 | Parallel Lives | Abundance | -Redundancy, +Flood protection |
| 4 | Enlightened Tutor | Fauna Shaman | +Creature, repeatable, rabbit |
| 5 | Boros Charm | Ephemerate | +Targeted evasion, flicker synergy |

**Net change:** 5 cards, but all high-impact replacements.

---

## Thematic Considerations

- **Fauna Shaman** is a rabbit (art + mechanic alignment) — better fit than tutors
- **Green Sun's Zenith** / **Ephemerate** are still in-color and on-theme (green instant-speed tools)
- **Mox Opal** sacrifice-to-fast-mana fits "VIP party" concept just as well as Chrome Mox
- **Abundance** thematically works (bounty, harvest table never emptying)
- These changes *raise* average theme score by replacing generic tutors with creatures/effects tied to the party concept

---

## Testing Recommendations

1. **Hands simulation:** Do these changes smooth out mana consistency in early turns?
2. **Mid-game:** Does Fauna Shaman + Survival of the Fittest create the tutor chain it's supposed to?
3. **Late-game:** Does Abundance + Doubling Season/Anointed Procession handle mana flooding?
4. **Protection:** Is Ephemerate + Teferi's Protection enough redundancy for board safety?
