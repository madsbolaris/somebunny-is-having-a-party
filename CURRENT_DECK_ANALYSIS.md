## PHASE 1: Strategic Foundation Assessment

### 1.1 Archetype Identification

**Primary Strategy Type**: Tribal / Go-Wide Tokens — rabbit tribal with token-doubler payoffs.

**Primary Win Conditions**:
1. **Combat — Go-Wide Overrun**: Flood the board with rabbit tokens (12× Hare Apparent, Tempt with Bunnies, Second Harvest, Hop to It, March of the Multitudes, Season of the Burrow) and finish with anthem stack (**Coat of Arms**, Cathars' Crusade, Beastmaster Ascension, Jetmir, Regal Bunnicorn) + an overrun (Craterhoof Behemoth, Finale of Devastation).
2. **Coat of Arms blowout**: With 8+ rabbits in play, every rabbit gets +N/+N — frequently lethal across the table in one swing.
3. **Hour of Reckoning + Second Harvest** asymmetric reset — wipe non-tokens, double survivors, swing.

**Secondary / Backup Win Conditions**:
- **Halo Fountain** alt-win (tap 5 untapped creatures three times — easy with token swarm + Valley Questcaller / Jetmir vigilance).
- Commander damage / value grind via Baylen's draw-on-bounce trigger.

**Commander's Role**: **Important** — Baylen is the centerpiece engine but the deck still functions without him because Hare Apparent + doublers + Coat of Arms is self-sufficient.

**Coherence**: Very focused. Nearly every non-utility slot is a token producer, anthem/doubler, or scaling payoff.

---

### 1.2 Speed and Consistency

**Average Win Turn (goldfish)**: **Turn 7–8**, occasionally Turn 6 with a doubler + Cradle / Henge nut draw.

**Game Stage Capabilities**:
- **T1–3**: Sol Ring / Signet / Talisman / Birds of Paradise / Three Visits / Nature's Lore — strong T1 dork or T2 ramp into T3 Baylen / token producer.
- **T4–6**: Resolve a doubler (Anointed Procession, Parallel Lives, Doubling Season) or anthem (Cathars' Crusade, **Coat of Arms**, Beastmaster Ascension, Jetmir); draw via Tocasia's Welcome, Welcoming Vampire, Mentor of the Meek, Skullclamp, Caretaker's Talent.
- **T7+**: Craterhoof / Finale / Tempt with Bunnies / Halo Fountain close.

**Consistency**:
- **Tutors: 6** (Chord of Calling, Eladamri's Call, Sylvan Tutor, Green Sun's Zenith, Natural Order, Finale of Devastation).
- Redundancy: 12× Hare Apparent, three independent token doublers, four anthems (now including Coat of Arms), two overrun finishers, multiple "double tokens this turn" effects.
- Card draw: ~10 sources (Tocasia's Welcome, Welcoming Vampire, Mentor of the Meek, Skullclamp, Sylvan Library, The Great Henge, Carrot Cake, Caretaker's Talent, Shamanic Revelation, Halo Fountain) + Smothering Tithe treasures.
- Mulligan quality: forgiving — any opener with a 1-CMC ramp + a Hare Apparent + lands is a keep.

---

### 1.3 Game Plan Documentation

**Ideal Sequence**:
- **T1**: Land + Sol Ring / Birds of Paradise / Hare Apparent.
- **T2**: Land + Signet / Talisman / Three Visits / Nature's Lore + Hare Apparent #2 or Welcoming Vampire.
- **T3**: Baylen, the Haymaker (or a doubler).
- **T4**: Doubler (Anointed Procession / Parallel Lives) or **Coat of Arms** → cast Hop to It / Tempt with Bunnies for an explosive board.
- **T5–6**: Cathars' Crusade / Jetmir / Beastmaster Ascension on board, swing for very large damage.
- **T7+**: GSZ → Craterhoof, Finale of Devastation X=large for lethal, or Halo Fountain alt-win.

**Critical Mass**: ~8 rabbits + Coat of Arms = lethal. Or ~10 rabbits + any anthem + haste/trample.

**Pivot Points**: "Go bigger" (Doubling Season + Hare Apparent ETBs) → "go faster" (Hour of Reckoning + Second Harvest) → "alt-win" (Halo Fountain).

### Phase 1 Strategy Summary

This Baylen, the Haymaker deck is a **focused go-wide rabbit-tribal token deck** that wins on **turns 7–8** primarily through combat damage amplified by token doublers (Anointed Procession, Parallel Lives, Doubling Season), tribal anthems (**Coat of Arms**, Cathars' Crusade, Jetmir, Beastmaster Ascension), and overrun finishers (Craterhoof Behemoth, Finale of Devastation). The 12-Hare-Apparent build creates absurd redundancy, and Baylen's bounce-and-draw trigger gives the deck both protection from board wipes and steady card flow. Halo Fountain is a credible alt-win backup. Main weakness is reliance on resolving a creature-payoff into combat — a well-timed Cyclonic Rift or Farewell can set the deck back several turns.

## Final Summary

**Key Strengths**:
1. **Extreme redundancy** — 12× Hare Apparent + 3 doublers + 4 anthems (now including Coat of Arms) + 2 overrun finishers = essentially un-disruptable at the card-pool level.
2. **Premium mana base** at 31 lands, ~97% Tier 1–2 lands, 6 strong tutors.
3. **Strong protection suite** (Heroic Intervention, Flawless Maneuver, Boros Charm, Unbreakable Formation, Grand Crescendo) defending the go-wide turn.

**Key Weaknesses**:
1. **No 0-CMC fast mana** (Bracket 3 caps you out of Mox Diamond, Chrome Mox, Mana Crypt, Jeweled Lotus).
2. **No stack interaction** — vulnerable to combo opponents and to counters on Craterhoof / Finale.
3. **No graveyard recursion** — repeated wipes / Farewell are very painful.

## Additional On-Theme Swap Recommendations (B3-Legal)

These improvements stay rabbit/token themed and respect the **Bracket 3 cap of 3 Game Changers** (the deck is already at the cap with Natural Order, Smothering Tithe, and Gaea's Cradle — so no new Game Changers can be added without crossing into Bracket 4).

### High-confidence (clear upgrade)

| Cut | Add | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Jacked Rabbit** | **Bennie Bracks, Zoologist** | Bennie IS a rabbit, draws a card whenever a 1/1 token enters — fuels the Hare Apparent + token doubler engine. Strict more-on-theme upgrade. *(Not a Game Changer.)* |
| **Sunbaked Canyon** | **basic Plains** | Sunbaked is a "pay 1 life to draw 1" cycle land — costs you tempo. A basic is a better fetch target for your 6 fetchlands. *(Not a Game Changer.)* |

### Medium-confidence (situational / playstyle)

| Cut | Add | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Mentor of the Meek** | **Bramble Sovereign** | Mentor needs mana per draw. Bramble Sovereign copies a Hare Apparent ETB to make a token rabbit copy — explosive with Anointed Procession / Parallel Lives. (Trade-off: Bramble costs 2G to use; Mentor is purer card draw.) |
| **Spire Garden** | **Wooded Bastion** OR another fetch target | Spire Garden enters tapped without 2 opponents revealing lands — flaky in many openers. A filter land is more reliable. |

### Lower-confidence (bigger philosophy shifts)

| Cut | Add | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Pollen-Shield Hare** | **Heraldic Banner (rabbit)** | Pollen-Shield is one-shot protection. Heraldic Banner is anthem + ramp for rabbits, repeatable. (Loses a creature body, hurts Natural Order.) |
| **Carrot Cake** | **Vanquisher's Banner (rabbit)** | Carrot Cake is cute but slow. Vanquisher's Banner draws on every rabbit cast. *(User has previously declined cutting Carrot Cake — leaving as a flag.)* |

### Recommended priority order

If you only do two more swaps:
1. **−Jacked Rabbit / +Bennie Bracks, Zoologist** (more on-theme creature, draws on tokens)
2. **−Sunbaked Canyon / +basic Plains** (clean land upgrade)

*(Keep Shamanic Revelation — in this token-heavy deck it draws far more than Return of the Wildspeaker, which only counts highest-power creatures and would exclude all your 1/1 rabbit tokens.)*

Estimated impact: 7.5 → ~7.7. All Bracket 3, no new Game Changers.
