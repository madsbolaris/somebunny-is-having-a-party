# Somebunny Is Having a Party

A little family of rabbit-flavored Commander decks.

They all begin the same way—*somebunny’s having a party*—but no two parties end alike. One overruns the meadow with an adorable stampede. One drags her wedding guests around the world until their bank accounts empty. One serves a magnificent feast where the guests are also the ingredients.

Choose your host carefully.

## The Hosts

Every host throws a different kind of party. Each deck lives in its own folder under [decks/](decks/), along with a `party.toml` manifest, decklists, theme analysis, and generated card mosaics.

### 🎉 Baylen, the Haymaker — *Somebunny Brought the Whole Warren*

[Baylen, the Haymaker](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/345/baylen-the-haymaker) meant to mark the [Second Harvest](https://scryfall.com/card/blc/123/second-harvest) in the [Season of the Burrow](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/29/season-of-the-burrow) with a quaint party beneath [The Party Tree](https://scryfall.com/card/ltc/348). But somebunny had other ideas.

By afternoon, [Harvestrite Host](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/15/harvestrite-host) has transformed the clearing into a [Utopia Sprawl](https://scryfall.com/card/woc/135/utopia-sprawl) of lanterns, garlands, and long tables. Baylen’s [Caretaker’s Talent](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/6/caretakers-talent) promises to keep every guest fed, entertained, and thoroughly charmed. A [Tocasia’s Welcome](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/181/tocasias-welcome) awaits everyone at the entrance, and [Harvestrite Host](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/15/harvestrite-host) installs a [Halo Fountain](https://scryfall.com/card/snc/285/halo-fountain) at the center of it all, a gleaming promise that the night will end in triumph.

And then the guests arrive. [Hare Apparent](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/15/hare-apparent) brings nine suspiciously similar plus-ones, [Warren Warleader](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/38/warren-warleader) charges in with more rabbits at her heels, and [Head of the Homestead](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/216/head-of-the-homestead) appears with an entire entourage. [Valley Questcaller](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/36/valley-questcaller) welcomes every newcomer like the guest of honor, [Rabbit Battery](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/157/rabbit-battery) sends [Jacked Rabbit](https://scryfall.com/card/blc/9/jacked-rabbit) charging straight onto the dance floor, and [Regal Bunnicorn](https://scryfall.com/card/woe/25/regal-bunnicorn) stands quietly in the corner becoming alarmingly large. [Enduring Vitality](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/176/enduring-vitality) and [Seedborn Muse](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/268/seedborn-muse) keep everybunny dancing long after sensible rabbits have gone to bed, while [Clever Concealment](https://scryfall.com/card/plst/ONC-5/clever-concealment) keeps the party out of sight.

But word spreads. An [Eladamri’s Call](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/197/eladamris-call) joins the [Chord of Calling](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/134/chord-of-calling) rolling across the hills, carrying news that the party of the season is already underway. Someone shouts [Assemble the Legion](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Assemble+the+Legion%22), every neighboring warren takes it personally, and one cry of [Hop to It](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/16/hop-to-it) returns as a [March of the Multitudes](https://scryfall.com/card/ncc/346/march-of-the-multitudes). [Anointed Procession](https://scryfall.com/card/akh/2/anointed-procession), [Doubling Season](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/216/doubling-season), [Mondrak, Glory Dominus](https://scryfall.com/card/one/23/mondrak-glory-dominus), and [Ojer Taq, Deepest Foundation](https://scryfall.com/card/lci/26/ojer-taq-deepest-foundation) ensure that every arrival brings several more rabbits no one remembers inviting. The guest list becomes less of a list and more of a regional census.

For one shining moment, Baylen looks across the clearing and decides this must be [Faith’s Reward](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Faith%27s+Reward%22): every neighboring warren gathered beneath the lanterns, dancing together as though the night might never end. Every newcomer insists on paying their share with a [Smothering Tithe](https://scryfall.com/card/p22/5/smothering-tithe), burying the gift tables beneath pies, flowers, blankets, and carrots. [Impact Tremors](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/717) shake the rafters with every arrival, and the celebration swells toward a [Grand Crescendo](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/118/grand-crescendo).

By midnight, however, the buffet is empty, the tables are buckling, and no one can remember who was invited. Good manners give way to [Survival of the Fittest](https://scryfall.com/card/exo/129/survival-of-the-fittest), while the search for the final carrot cake slice becomes a [Cathars’ Crusade](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/17/cathars-crusade) sweeping from table to table. Rabbits climb the garlands, defend abandoned plates, and claim entire sections of the dance floor as sovereign territory. The bells, drums, and thousands of dancing paws tighten into a [Skullclamp](https://scryfall.com/card/blc/283/skullclamp) around Baylen’s temples.

The chaos begins to move as one and the celebration becomes a [Beastmaster Ascension](https://scryfall.com/card/ncc/283/beastmaster-ascension) as thousands of paws fall into a perfect [Pawpatch Formation](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/186/pawpatch-formation), and the shouting becomes a roar. The roar becomes a charge.

And then the last invitation is answered. The [Finale of Devastation](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/289/finale-of-devastation) brings [Craterhoof Behemoth](https://scryfall.com/card/tdm/404/craterhoof-behemoth) crashing through the tree line. One stomp sets the whole warren in motion, and nothing between the dance floor and the horizon survives.

**Everybunny hopped in. Everybunny trampled out.**

### 💍 Ms. Bumbleflower — *Somebunny Has to Pay for This*

Ms. Bumbleflower has found the one. She has only one demand: everybunny *must* be there to [Savor the Moment](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/1006) when she says “I do.” Not at just one ceremony, of course—that would never do. She wants memories to last a lifetime, so she books the whole world.

Everybunny enjoys the first few ceremonies, raising a toast to a love as enduring as [Teferi’s Ageless Insight](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/119). After all, Ms. Bumbleflower promised there would only be [Three Visits](https://scryfall.com/card/clb/837/three-visits). But one [Rhystic Study](https://scryfall.com/card/j22/114) soon becomes a full thesis: a little [Exploration](https://scryfall.com/card/cns/164) reveals another coastline, a [Burgeoning](https://scryfall.com/card/cn2/178) itinerary spreads across the map, and every [Farseek](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/304/farseek) appears to [Open the Way](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/189/open-the-way) to somewhere new. [Seedborn Muse](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/194/seedborn-muse) only makes matters worse, filling the margins with increasingly ambitious destinations.

Before long, the itinerary has doubled, and Ms. Bumbleflower needs an entire team to keep it running. [Kwain, Itinerant Meddler](https://scryfall.com/card/cmr/284) handles the logistics, [Mangara, the Diplomat](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/155) negotiates the borders, and [Selvala, Explorer Returned](https://scryfall.com/card/cn2/70/selvala-explorer-returned) leads the increasingly exhausted tours. [Teferi, Time Raveler](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Teferi%2C+Time+Raveler%22) keeps the schedule moving. [Bruvac the Grandiloquent](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/35), the officiant, delivers the same [Gaea’s Blessing](https://scryfall.com/card/dom/161) for the eighteenth time, only louder, while Ms. Bumbleflower accepts her [Wedding Ring](https://scryfall.com/card/who/1059) *again*—although by now even she suspects it is just [The One Ring](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/246) being passed around.

The [Collective Voyage](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/149/collective-voyage) carries them from vows beneath the [Riverchurn Monument](https://scryfall.com/card/dft/57) to a reception at the feet of the [Consecrated Sphinx](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/165), each stop leaving everybunny more dazzled—and more exhausted—than the last. Champagne pours from the [Font of Mythos](https://scryfall.com/card/con/136), the [Temple Bell](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/277) rings, and the [Rites of Flourishing](https://scryfall.com/card/blc/122) never seem to end. But every new entry in the [Folio of Fancies](https://scryfall.com/card/eld/46) only feeds the [Horn of Greed](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/385/horn-of-greed), and [Forced Fruition](https://scryfall.com/card/lrw/66) ensures that each extravagance leads to seven more. Soon, even a [Windfall](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/123) cannot cover the [Smothering Tithe](https://scryfall.com/card/2x2/342) quietly draining their accounts, one destination at a time.

The guests begin to fade. Ms. Bumbleflower simply restocks. [Reliquary Tower](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/398) warehouses the presents, [Hall of Heliod’s Generosity](https://scryfall.com/card/mh1/241/hall-of-heliods-generosity) restores whatever she has already spent, and one well-timed [Replenish](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Replenish%22) rebuilds the entire celebration before anyone can escape through the rubble. One sip from the [Elixir of Immortality](https://scryfall.com/card/c21/243) leaves her refreshed, radiant, and ready to renew the vows.

As the bills mount, [Trouble in Pairs](https://scryfall.com/card/mkc/15) keeps the couples too busy arguing to compare receipts. Those who object are met with a [Counterspell](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Counterspell%22); those who persist watch their complaints disappear into a [Mana Drain](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Mana+Drain%22). Even a coordinated revolt is dismissed by a [Force of Negation](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Force+of+Negation%22), followed by [An Offer You Can’t Refuse](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/160). Ms. Bumbleflower has no patience for such practical concerns; to her, it is simply [Mind Over Matter](https://scryfall.com/card/exo/40/mind-over-matter).

Should the guests become too difficult, a [Cyclonic Rift](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/84) sweeps the entire wedding party to its most remote destination: a chapel carved into the [Glacial Chasm](https://scryfall.com/card/ice/353). From a stained-glass window above the altar, [Jace, Wielder of Mysteries](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/506/jace-wielder-of-mysteries) watches the [Approach of the Second Sun](https://scryfall.com/card/akh/4/approach-of-the-second-sun) with the expression of someone who knows they will all be doing this again tomorrow. The guests are too cold to lift a paw in protest.

The vows blur into another turn of [Teferi’s Puzzle Box](https://scryfall.com/card/10e/340/teferis-puzzle-box), while the bells, speeches, and travel announcements become a [Maddening Cacophony](https://scryfall.com/card/znr/67). As [Psychic Corrosion](https://scryfall.com/card/m19/68) sets in, the guests lose track of where they have been, what they agreed to, and how many pages remain in their checkbooks. By the time the last bill arrives, all they can afford is one final [Swan Song](https://scryfall.com/card/c16/98).

There simply isn’t anything left.

Everybunny came to celebrate. Everybunny left bankrupt.

**Till deck do us part.**


* **Deck type:** Bant group-hug mill and draw punishment
* **Bracket:** 4 — Optimized (Game-Changer-dense, but a slow clock; not cEDH)
* **Good against:** Slow midrange and control decks, draw-heavy strategies, and decks that aggressively tutor through their libraries
* **Struggles against:** Graveyard decks that benefit from being milled, shuffle effects that restore the library, draw-denial effects, and fast combo decks that exploit the extra cards
* **Folder:** [decks/bumbleflower/](decks/bumbleflower/)
* **Decklist:** [decks/bumbleflower/decklist_b4.dck](decks/bumbleflower/decklist_b4.dck)

### 🍽️ Finneas, Ace Archer — *Somebunny’s on the Menu*

Beneath [Scurry Oak](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/172), a [Chord of Calling](https://scryfall.com/card/rvr/134/chord-of-calling) invites everybunny to the greatest feast the meadow has ever seen. [Finneas, Ace Archer](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/348) arrives to find [Caretaker’s Talent](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/6) on full display, with [Rosie Cotton of South Lane](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/27) filling plates and [Quina, Qu Gourmet](https://scryfall.com/card/fin/194) tasting everything twice.

A [Gilded Goose](https://scryfall.com/card/blc/83) lays the first course while a [Tireless Provisioner](https://scryfall.com/card/ltc/262) keeps the platters coming. The [Witch’s Oven](https://scryfall.com/card/eld/237) and [Academy Manufactor](https://scryfall.com/card/otc/251) produce more than any kitchen reasonably should, while [Peregrin Took](https://scryfall.com/card/ltr/181) discovers an extra helping beside every helping. A magnificent [Carrot Cake](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/7) waits beneath glass, [Stocking the Pantry](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/194) keeps every dish full, and a [Spike Feeder](https://scryfall.com/card/cmd/172) has somehow fallen asleep in the fruit bowl.

The hospitality remains impeccable even as [Placid Rottentail](https://scryfall.com/card/mom/199) begins carrying abandoned coats toward the pantry. [Byrke, Long Ear of the Law](https://scryfall.com/card/blb/380) opens [The Case of the Uneaten Feast](https://scryfall.com/card/mkm/10), but a [Herd Baloth](https://scryfall.com/card/mh2/165/herd-baloth) makes it clear that the kitchen is not accepting visitors.

Behind it, [Ashnod’s Altar](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Ashnod%27s+Altar%22) waits beneath a dangling [Skullclamp](https://scryfall.com/card/tdc/103), while carts from the [High Market](https://scryfall.com/card/soc/380) arrive full and depart empty. The adjoining [Field of the Dead](https://scryfall.com/card/m20/247) keeps the pantry stocked, and [Of Herbs and Stewed Rabbit](https://scryfall.com/card/ltc/17) reveals why the invitation promised room for everybunny.

[Bess, Soul Nourisher](https://scryfall.com/card/pncc/67p) politely gathers the remaining guests while [Kodama of the West Tree](https://scryfall.com/card/neo/199) closes every woodland path and [Ajani, Strength of the Pride](https://scryfall.com/card/m20/2) guards the doors. [Soul’s Attendant](https://scryfall.com/card/roe/44) welcomes each rabbit forward, only to pass the [Hinterland Sanctifier](https://scryfall.com/card/fdn/730) into the waiting hands of [Emmara, Soul of the Accord](https://scryfall.com/card/grn/168). [Loran’s Escape](https://scryfall.com/card/bro/14), it turns out, was never meant for the diners.

[Enduring Vitality](https://scryfall.com/card/dsk/176/enduring-vitality) keeps every offering distressingly fresh while [Heliod, Sun-Crowned](https://scryfall.com/card/cmm/29) blesses the feast as a [Cathars’ Crusade](https://scryfall.com/card/inr/17). An [Ocelot Pride](https://scryfall.com/card/mh3/38/ocelot-pride) waits beneath the table for scraps as the [Archangel of Thune](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/1337) descends to bless dessert.

Finneas finally draws his bow, but [Silence](https://scryfall.com/search?q=%21%22Silence%22) falls as the [Cordyceps Rat King](https://scryfall.com/card/sld/2205) emerges to greet his guests. Everybunny came for dinner; not everybunny understood the invitation.

**Bunny appétit.**


* **Deck type:** Selesnya tokens, +1/+1 counters, lifegain, and creature combo
* **Bracket:** 4 — Optimized, high end (real compact combos like Heliod + Spike Feeder)
* **Good against:** Aggressive and damage-based decks, fair creature strategies, and decks relying on spot removal to control the board
* **Struggles against:** Repeated exile or bounce sweepers, effects that prevent lifegain or counters, pillow-fort strategies, and faster noncombat combo decks
* **Folder:** [decks/finneas/](decks/finneas/)
* **Decklist:** [decks/finneas/decklist_b4.dck](decks/finneas/decklist_b4.dck)

## Repository Layout

```
decks/
  <host>/
    party.toml            # host manifest (host, commander, brackets, mosaic rules)
    decklist_<bracket>.dck # Forge decklist per bracket
    decklist_<bracket>.txt / _moxfield.txt  # generated plain-text exports
    theme_analysis.md     # per-card theme evaluation for this host
    deck_mosaic_<bracket>.png / _preview.jpg  # generated card mosaics
```

| Path | Description |
|------|-------------|
| [decks/](decks/) | One folder per host, each a self-contained party |
| [decks/baylen/](decks/baylen/) | Baylen, the Haymaker — Naya rabbit tribal (B4) |
| [decks/bumbleflower/](decks/bumbleflower/) | Ms. Bumbleflower — Bant forced-draw mill (B4) |
| [decks/finneas/](decks/finneas/) | Finneas, Ace Archer — The Potluck: Selesnya Food, tokens & counters/lifegain (B4) |
| [scripts/generate_mosaic.py](scripts/generate_mosaic.py) | Generates mosaics for every host (`python scripts/generate_mosaic.py [host] [bracket]`) |
| [scripts/dck_to_text.py](scripts/dck_to_text.py) | Exports decklists to plain text |
| [scripts/dck_to_moxfield.py](scripts/dck_to_moxfield.py) | Exports decklists to Moxfield/Archidekt set-code format |
| [card_images/](card_images/) | Shared cache of card images from Scryfall |
| [.github/workflows/update-mosaics.yml](.github/workflows/update-mosaics.yml) | Automatically regenerates and commits deck images on card changes |
